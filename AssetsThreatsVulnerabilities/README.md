# Assets, Threats, and Vulnerabilities

## Overview
This folder contains risk modeling, vulnerability assessments, and mitigation strategies designed to protect organizational assets from emerging threat vectors.

---

## 📄 Key Projects & Analyses
* **Vulnerability Assessment Report:** Analyzed system vulnerabilities using CVE/NVD databases and prioritized remediation based on CVSS severity scores.
* **Threat Modeling & Attack Surface Analysis:** Mapped out potential attack vectors, social engineering threats, and unpatched software flaws.
* **Asset Classification & Protection Plan:** Categorized critical enterprise data and defined baseline security controls to maintain confidentiality, integrity, and availability (CIA Triad).

* Vulnerability Management & CVSS Scoring
* Threat Modeling & Attack Vector Identification
* CIA Triad & Asset Protection
* Cryptographic Controls & Hashing Baselines
